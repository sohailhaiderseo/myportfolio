Frequently asked questions

What is an SEO audit?

A full check of your website. I look for slow pages, broken links, or missing titles, then give you a list of fixes, most important first.

What is Local SEO?

It helps people near you find your business on Google and Google Maps, using your Google Business Profile, local keywords, listings, and reviews.

How long does SEO take?

Small fixes can help in a few weeks. Bigger results usually take a few months, depending on competition and where you start.

Can you help me show up on Google Maps?

Yes. I improve what you can control: profile, website, and reviews. Google decides the final order, so no one can promise a spot.

What do you need from me?

Your website link, access to your Google Business Profile, a list of your services, and the places you serve.

Do you promise #1 rankings?

No. Nobody honest can. I promise clear work, simple reports, and steady effort.
